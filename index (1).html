Overview

From raw tweets to a verdict

Misinformation spread fast during the pandemic. This study benchmarks transfer-learning transformers against LLMs on a public tweet dataset, then combines model outputs through fusion and numerical optimization.

CollectCOVID-19 English tweets
CleanStrip URLs, tags, numbers; lowercase
Fine-tune4 transformers + 3 LLMs
Fuse6 fusion / optimization methods
EvaluateAcc · P · R · F1 · confusion
Dataset

COVID-19 English Tweets

A public dataset with two columns, Tweets (text) and Labels (real / fake). Classes are close to balanced, so no resampling was needed.

Class distribution

Real · 3,360Fake · 3,060

Data split

Train 70%Validation 10%Test 20%

Preprocessing

  • Removed URLs and special tags
  • Removed numbers
  • Normalised whitespace
  • Lowercased all text
  • Stratification unnecessary — classes near-balanced
Models

What we compared

Seven pretrained models across two families, plus six ways to combine their predictions.

Results

GPT-2 leads on the test set

GPT-2 was the best single model, reaching about 0.96 across every metric.
